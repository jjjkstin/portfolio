I was one of **38 students admitted nationwide** (under a 10% acceptance rate) to the **Princeton Summer Journalism Program (PSJP)**, a year-long, fully funded journalism program at Princeton University. The program is tuition-free and offered on full scholarship.

- Learned from Princeton University professors and from journalists at _The New York Times_, the _Associated Press_, _Bloomberg_, and other newsrooms.
- Reported, wrote, and published the **2025 _Princeton Summer Journal_** alongside my cohort.

**Reference:** [2025 _Princeton Summer Journal_ (PDF)](https://psjp.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf2456/files/documents/2025%20Princeton%20Summer%20Journal_0.pdf)
