In the summer of 2025, I was a scholar at **Yale Young Global Scholars (YYGS)**, a highly selective summer program (about a 15% acceptance rate) held on Yale University's campus.

- Attended lectures by Yale University professors.
- Conducted a research project on **humanitarian issues facing migrant workers**, with guidance from Yale mentors.
