**Koread** is a youth-led digital media platform dedicated to delivering local and global news about and for Korean-Americans. I founded it in 2023 and led it as Executive Director through 2026.

Our team is made up of talented students around the globe who bring their diverse experiences and perspectives to our content, through journalism, editorials, and visual media. Koread aims to connect people and communities of various ethnic backgrounds by raising awareness of important issues, amplifying underrepresented voices, and fostering meaningful discussions.

The platform welcomes a wide range of topics that reflect the unique and evolving experiences of the Korean-American community. With contributors in different parts of the world, Koread covers both local and global news, highlighting the stories that matter to our readers, and is committed to a supportive, inclusive community where diverse voices and perspectives can thrive.

### My role

- Founded and led a Korean-American news platform with **18 team members across three continents**, covering political, economic, and cultural issues.
- Oversaw editorial strategy and operations, generating **40,000+ views** across published reporting and commentary.
