An independent qualitative case study of a DACA recipient's journey in the United States, published in the _National High School Journal of Science_ (2025).

Drawing on a semi-structured interview and community cultural wealth theory, the study identifies four themes: immigration journey and status awareness, educational barriers and resilience, identity formation and cultural integration, and policy perspectives and future efforts.
