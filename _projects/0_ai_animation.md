_In progress._

An independent research paper on AI-generated animation and its implications for art, including how audiences assign emotional value to AI-generated artworks, and what that could mean for creative work in the future.

This project connects to my broader interest in **AI governance and AI policymaking**: how societies decide the role that AI-generated work should play, and how policy can respond.
