Navigating Race Project

Research Intern, The SLAAY Lab, Montclair State University

From June to December 2024, I was a research intern for Dr. Sei Eun Kim at The SLAAY Lab, Montclair State University, working on the Navigating Race Project, which studies the experiences of Asian and Latino parents and youth.

  • Conducted literature reviews and synthesized findings to inform the research design.
  • Assisted with participant recruitment and with developing study protocols, surveys, and interview guides.
  • Supported quantitative research in R, collaborating with graduate students and faculty.

Reference: The SLAAY Lab
